# domain must not read ambient state. The school-year boundary and every audit timestamp are
# resolved by callers and passed in; a clock read here would be untestable and, for the September
# boundary, timezone-ambiguous (DEC-12).
P:System.DateTime.Now;Use TimeProvider, injected by the caller.
P:System.DateTime.UtcNow;Use TimeProvider, injected by the caller.
P:System.DateTimeOffset.Now;Use TimeProvider, injected by the caller.
P:System.DateTimeOffset.UtcNow;Use TimeProvider, injected by the caller.
P:System.DateTime.Today;Use TimeProvider and the school timezone (DEC-12).
